\section{Mechanistic Experiments and Diagnostics}
\label{app:mechanistic}

\subsection{Replay as a dynamical clue}
\label{app:replay-clue}

The temporal clue in Section~\ref{sec:sharpening} concerns the total online gap, not a constant increase in novel-continuation loss. In the seed-42 both-table run \code{s1v5_128_ep1xL4_20ep_both_fixed}, with the standard input-injection setting and 337 steps per pass, the gaps at the ends of passes 1--4 (steps 337, 674, 1011 and 1348) are $-0.062$, $1.199$, $2.730$ and $3.917$ nats. The first three replay increments are therefore $1.261$, $1.531$ and $1.187$ nats: comparable early increments, rather than an exact constant. The increments subsequently decline, reaching $0.241$ nats from pass 19 to 20. The essential observation is that the same missing continuations remain unobserved on every pass while the model's gap keeps growing. This motivates a dynamical account of the model response; it does not by itself distinguish optimization mechanisms.

\subsection{Sharpening diagnostics}

Section~\ref{sec:sharpening} compares a context-only probe of ``walk their dog,'' whose training continuation is \emph{down} and whose fixed-validation continuations include \emph{daily} and \emph{for}, across replay passes (Figure~\ref{fig:walk-their-dog}). The aggregate diagnostic separates validation positions with a training-seen bigram context by whether their true continuation was also seen after that context in training, then compares the both-table model with the no-gram control (Figure~\ref{fig:crowd-ce}). Predictive entropy and cross-entropy are computed on the same fixed validation positions so that sharper distributions can be distinguished from improved accuracy.

For the displayed context, \emph{down} occurs once after the trigram in training; \emph{daily} and \emph{for} occur once each in the fixed validation window and not after this trigram in training. Panel (a) uses the seed-42 \path{sharpening_f99_contextonly_20260921_s42_fixed} context probe. At the ends of five passes, steps 337, 674, 1011, 1348, and 1685, it presents only the literal context and reads its full-vocabulary next-token distribution. Its training loss is $-\log p(\textit{down})$; the validation loss is the mean of $-\log p(\textit{daily})$ and $-\log p(\textit{for})$. Neither is the original full-prefix online loss. This one example illustrates crowding-out; it is not a claim that every context sharpens monotonically.

Panel (b) instead uses full causal prefixes and position-weighted validation cross-entropy. The archived aggregation \path{fig6_global_seen_novel_20260922_s42_fixed} plots the original both-table checkpoints and a separately evaluated no-gram control at the same five epoch ends. Of the 589{,}248 classified validation positions, 405{,}058 have a training-seen continuation and 158{,}790 a novel continuation after a training-seen bigram context; 25{,}400 contexts absent from training are excluded from these two curves. The entropy comparison also concerns fixed validation positions, not the context-only illustration. The two panels have different inputs and estimands and should not be read as a matched per-example loss decomposition.

\subsection{Context-loss masking}

The experiment in Section~\ref{sec:context-locality} excludes next-token loss for a random 2\% of training trigram contexts beginning at epoch 2 or 3, while retaining their input tokens, trainable parameters, and the loss denominator. A disjoint 2\% of unmasked contexts and a normal-training run from the same checkpoint are controls. Figure~\ref{fig:ctxmask-gap} compares the masked and peer sets using $C_S=\sum_{i\in S}q_i[\bar\ell_{\mathrm{val},i}-\bar\ell_{\mathrm{tr},i}]$ on fixed batches. Figure~\ref{fig:ctxmask-mass} tracks the probability assigned to each context's training-seen continuations in both the n-gram model and the no-gram control. The contrast tests whether removing a context's own updates stops its gap growth despite continued optimization on other contexts; it does not assume mathematically independent parameter subspaces.

An independently archived one-pass audit makes the cohort and measurement choices explicit. It samples 2\% of exact training trigram contexts uniformly without replacement (selection seed 20260919); among 379{,}789 selected contexts, 5{,}565 have fixed-validation support (8{,}083 positions). A disjoint unmasked peer sample has 5{,}571 measurable contexts (8{,}082 positions), not the entire unmasked complement. In that audit, $q_i=N_{\mathrm{val},i}/589{,}824$ uses the original fixed-validation position count without renormalizing within either cohort. Per-context train and validation losses preserve their full causal prefixes at a common checkpoint, unlike the context-only sharpening probe. The epoch-2 pair is \path{ctxloss_e2_all_p02_control_s42_fixed} and \path{ctxloss_e2_all_p02_mask_s42_fixed} over steps 337--674; the separate epoch-3 pair is \path{ctxloss_all_p02_control_s42_fixed} and \path{ctxloss_all_p02_mask_s42_fixed} over steps 674--1011. Within each pair, normal and masked training begin from the same checkpoint, but these are separate experiments and are not the four-epoch trajectories displayed in Figure~\ref{fig:ctxmask-gap}. Suppressed aggregate growth on selected contexts alongside continued growth on peers supports approximate locality of additional gap accumulation. Individual losses still move in both directions, and a pre-existing gap can persist.

\subsection{Status of the context-masking comparison}
\label{app:mask-reconciliation}

The original trajectories in Figure~\ref{fig:context-subspace-mask} suggest that removing a context's own training loss arrests its gap growth. Registered attempts to reproduce this pattern under the minimal setting, however, did not recover the arrest with either context-type masking or table-row masking. In the seed-42 input both-table runs \code{s1v5_128_marm_both_rowmask_ctrl_fixed} and \code{s1v5_128_marm_both_rowmask_e1_fixed}, masking starts at step 338; at step 670 the masked cohort's online gap contribution is $0.0353$ nats, compared with $0.0247$ in the unmasked control. With masking from step 675 in \code{s1v5_128_marm_both_rowmask_e2_fixed}, the corresponding step-790 contributions are $0.0551$ versus $0.0492$ nats. These runs retain the standard input setting, $2^{20}$ rows per table, RMSProp table LR $128\times$ and backbone LR $0.0006$.

The rerun quantity is the cohort's validation-position share multiplied by the difference between its validation mean and its online training mean. It is distinct from the matched fixed-probe quantity used in the original figure description. The figure's run provenance and the treatment of the loss denominator also need reconciliation. Thus these reruns are not a matched refutation of the displayed trajectories, but they prevent treating context independence as an established property of the current setting. Shared-backbone updates may continue to alter a masked context, and context-specific representations alone do not imply independent optimization.
